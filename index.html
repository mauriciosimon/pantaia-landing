Client work · Automotive resale platform

Dileo Seminuevos

Mobility & car-subscription company · Monterrey, MX · Pilot in production

Dileo is the single legal owner of every car it resells, with a complete service history and a proper invoice — a moat no used-car lot can copy. But the resale flow ran on WhatsApp threads through a chain of intermediaries, adding hours of latency to every offer.

We built the layer their commercial team actually uses: consigning clients and brokers transact directly, while Dileo's staff supervise instead of relaying messages.

  • Seller portal: market value, where it's published, offers, floor-price range
  • Broker catalog with comparable-price references and formal offers
  • Role-based access (admin, consultant, broker) over the inventory system of record
  • WhatsApp-first: offers, showings, odometer photos, weekly summaries

Before: a relay chain · After: direct, supervised
